FUND MANDATE -- ALPHA GROWTH FUND (F001)

Investment Objective
The Alpha Growth Fund seeks long-term capital appreciation by investing primarily
in large-cap equity securities listed on major US exchanges.

Investment Policy
- Minimum 80% allocation to US listed equities at all times
- Maximum 15% in any single stock (single-stock concentration limit)
- Minimum 2% cash reserve at all times
- No leverage or short positions permitted
- Derivatives may only be used for hedging purposes

Benchmark
S&P 500 Total Return Index

Risk Limits
- Tracking error target: 3-7% annualised vs benchmark
- Maximum drawdown alert: -15% from peak (requires board notification)
- Single stock limit: 15% of NAV
- Cash minimum: 2% of NAV

Reporting
Monthly NAV reports due by the 5th business day of the following month.
Quarterly risk reports due within 15 business days of quarter end.
Annual audited accounts due within 90 days of financial year end.

Effective Date: 1 January 2024
