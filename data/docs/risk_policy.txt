RISK MANAGEMENT POLICY

Scope
This policy applies to all funds managed by the firm including
Alpha Growth (F001), Beta Income (F002), and Gamma Balanced (F003).

Limit Breach Procedure
1. Any limit breach must be reported to the Risk Committee within 24 hours.
2. The portfolio manager must provide a remediation plan within 48 hours.
3. Breaches unresolved after 5 business days require board escalation.
4. All breaches are logged in the compliance system with timestamp and resolution date.

Limit Types
- MAX_WEIGHT_PCT : Maximum weight of any single position as % of NAV
- MIN_CASH_PCT   : Minimum cash holding as % of NAV
- MAX_DURATION   : Maximum portfolio duration in years (fixed income funds)
- MAX_EQUITY_PCT : Maximum equity exposure as % of NAV
- MAX_COMMODITY_PCT : Maximum commodity exposure as % of NAV

Escalation Matrix
- Warning (current within 90-100% of limit): notify portfolio manager
- Breach (current exceeds limit): notify Risk Committee + Compliance Officer
- Critical breach (current > 120% of limit): immediate board notification

Reporting Frequency
Daily limit monitoring reports are generated automatically.
Weekly risk summary distributed to senior management every Monday.
